Understanding Microplastics

Microplastics are very small plastic particles that are commonly defined as particles smaller than 5 millimeters. They may enter aquatic environments through different sources.

  • Breakdown of larger plastic waste
  • Synthetic textile fibers
  • Industrial activities
  • Plastic products and packaging
  • Urban and environmental pollution

Detection of potential microplastics can help researchers understand contamination patterns and support further laboratory investigation.
